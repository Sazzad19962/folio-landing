Questions

Will my PDF keep its layout?

Yes — if you just read it. Reflow only happens when you convert to EPUB or switch to reading mode, and you can flip back at any time.

Does Folio work with DRM-protected books?

Folio opens and converts DRM-free files only, and it never removes DRM. Books locked to a store such as Kindle or Kobo still read best in that store's app. Your own documents, papers, public-domain classics and DRM-free purchases all work in Folio.

Where are my files stored?

Files you convert without an account are processed over an encrypted connection and deleted automatically after 2 hours. You can also delete them right away from the download screen. Books you add to your library are encrypted in storage, are never shared or sold, and can be deleted for good at any time from your account settings.

Can I send books to my Kindle or Kobo?

Pro includes Send to e-reader. Add your device once and every conversion can go straight to it.
