    Formats

    FormatOpenSave asWhat carries over
    PDFYesYesBold, italic, underline, strikethrough, colours, fonts and sizes; alignment, indents and spacing; headings, lists, links, images and simple tables. Saved PDFs get a title page, page numbers and bookmarks.
    EPUBYesYesChapters, headings, title and author. Saved EPUBs work in Apple Books, Google Play Books, Kobo and Send to Kindle.
    DOCXYesYesSaved Word files keep the formatting read from PDFs: bold, underline, colours, fonts, alignment, indents, real bullet and numbered lists, images, tables, page size and page breaks. Opens in Word, Google Docs and LibreOffice.
    MarkdownYesYesHeadings and paragraphs; links and emphasis become plain text.
    HTMLYesYesHeadings, paragraphs and list items. Saved HTML is a single styled page.
    TXTYesYesParagraphs split on blank lines; hard-wrapped lines are joined so text can reflow.

    Good to know

    • Formatting, not exact positionsPDF to Word keeps bold, alignment, lists, images and simple tables. Multi-column pages, text boxes and complex tables become ordinary flowing paragraphs.
    • Scanned PDFsA PDF made of page photos has no text to read. It needs OCR first.
    • DRM-free onlyStore-locked ebooks can't be opened, and Folio never removes DRM.
    • বাংলা সমর্থিতBangla PDFs are written as real text in Noto Serif Bengali, with conjuncts and vowel signs shaped correctly. Other non-Latin scripts become page images in PDF; use EPUB or DOCX to keep them as text.
